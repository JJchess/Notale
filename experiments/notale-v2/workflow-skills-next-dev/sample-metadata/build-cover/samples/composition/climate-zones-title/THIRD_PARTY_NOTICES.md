## Map rendering and climate layer

The generated vector payload is derived from the upstream project's public Mapbox climate tileset. Visible Mapbox attribution is retained in the frame. No Mapbox token, style, tile, CDN, video, or other remote resource is requested at runtime.

The climate classification layer follows the upstream project's Köppen-Geiger presentation and its reference to Beck et al. (2018), *Present and future Köppen-Geiger climate classification maps at 1-km resolution*, Scientific Data 5, 180214. The vector adaptation is used here for visual reproduction; no claim of ownership is made over the underlying climate data.
