# Media quality gates

Reject or repair an asset when any check fails:

- The declared purpose is not visible in the result.
- Evidence lacks provenance or depicts the wrong object.
- Generated pixels contain critical text, pseudo-writing, formulas, data, logos, or watermarks.
- Crop, focal point, resolution, or aspect ratio cannot support the planned placement.
- A background lacks a readable safe zone or needs an overlay stronger than the content hierarchy can tolerate.
- Decoration enters the reading order, obscures content, competes with the focal point, or interferes with controls.
- The asset duplicates another page without a deliberate motif reason.
- The asset substitutes for state, execution, quantitative, relational, or geometric evidence.
