# Composition families

- `full-bleed-hero`: one dominant proposition or contextual image with generous native title space.
- `text-over-image`: native text over a controlled background safe zone and overlay.
- `cutout-split`: isolated object or person paired with a compact explanatory column.
- `annotated-specimen`: a faithful object with native callouts or a supporting diagram.
- `focal-object`: one object or native construct anchors a small set of relationships.
- `process-path`: ordered causal or procedural progression; use sim when states must be stepped.
- `before-after`: two states visible together with the changed evidence emphasized.
- `comparison`: balanced alternatives visible in the first frame.
- `experiment-setup`: apparatus, manipulated variable, measured output, and procedure hierarchy.
- `proof-equation-stage`: a derivation or proof dominates; supporting prose stays subordinate.
- `data-evidence`: chart/table plus conclusion and source/assumption.
- `collage`: several sourced artifacts with a single editorial reading path.
- `poster`: a background visual layer plus native headline and minimal supporting text.
- `research-figure`: figure, result, method cue, and limitation arranged for dense inspection.
- `interactive-stage`: sim or runnable receives the largest uninterrupted area and clear controls.

Do not repeat the same composition signature on adjacent pages unless the repetition expresses a deliberate comparison. Never sacrifice evidence legibility merely to increase variation.
